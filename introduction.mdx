## Overview

Collaborate To Deliver is a comprehensive SaaS platform designed for logistics and fleet operations. You manage parking areas, track vehicle statuses, and assign tasks seamlessly from a centralized dashboard. Optimize your operations by monitoring occupancy rates, maintenance needs, and deployments in real-time.

## Quick Start

Get up and running in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus" title-type="p">
    Visit `https://roos.collaborate-to-deliver.de` and create your account. Provide your logistics company details for tailored setup.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add First Parking Area" icon="plus" title-type="p">
    Navigate to "Parkbereiche" > "Neuer Parkbereich". Enter location (e.g., Ingolstadt), total spots (e.g., 16), and save.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitor Vehicles" icon="eye" title-type="p">
    View available areas. Check occupancy and spot statuses. Use "Save All" to apply bulk changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign Tasks" icon="list" title-type="p">
    Go to "Meine Aufgaben" to create and delegate fleet-related tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>
