## Overview

Workspaces help teams collaborate on projects.

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workspaces">
    Click **Workspaces** in the left menu after logging in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'New Workspace'">
    Enter a name and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Add their email addresses and click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start collaborating">
    Your workspace is ready for shared documents and tasks!
  </Step>
</Steps>
