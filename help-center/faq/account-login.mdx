## Overview

Log in to access project collaboration features.

## Steps

1. Visit `https://roos.collaborate-to-deliver.de/login`
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Login**

<Callout kind="tip">Forgot your password? Click "Forgot Password" on the login page to reset it.</Callout>

## Note

If your organization uses Single Sign-On (SSO), click **Sign in with SSO** and follow the prompts.
