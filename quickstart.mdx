## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Permissions to manage parking areas and vehicles in your organization
</Callout>

## Create and Verify Your Account

Follow these steps to sign up quickly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Sign-Up Page" icon="user-plus">
    Navigate to `https://roos.collaborate-to-deliver.de/signup`.

    Click **Create Account**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details" icon="edit-3">
    Provide your email, full name, and organization details.

    Set a strong password (at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for the verification email from Collaborate To Deliver.

    Click the verification link to activate your account.

    <Callout kind="tip">
      Didn't receive the email? Check your spam folder or request a new one from the sign-up page.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log In and Access the Dashboard

Once verified, log in to explore the platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Go to `https://roos.collaborate-to-deliver.de/login`.

    Enter your email and password.

    Click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the Dashboard" icon="layout-dashboard">
    You'll land on the main dashboard.

    Review the overview of your parking areas, tasks, and vehicles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Navigate the Main Interface

The dashboard provides quick access to key sections. Use the sidebar menu for navigation.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Parkbereiche" icon="map-pin">
    Manage your parking areas and occupancy.

    View available spots, occupancy rates (e.g., 94% in Ingolstadt), and statuses like Free, Ready, Service Due, In Maintenance, or Defective.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Meine Aufgaben" icon="check-square">
    Track your personal tasks related to vehicles and parking.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Fahrzeuge" icon="truck">
    Monitor vehicle statuses, maintenance schedules, and assignments.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Add Your First Parking Area

Set up a parking zone to start managing spots.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create New Area" icon="plus">
    From **Parkbereiche**, click **Neuer Parkbereich**.

    Enter details: Name (e.g., "Platz 1 Ingolstadt"), Location ("Ingolstadt"), Total Spots (e.g., 16).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Spots" icon="grid">
    Assign individual spot numbers (1-16).

    Set initial statuses using the legend (Frei, Einsatzbereit, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes" icon="save">
    Click **Save All** to apply.

    View occupancy and edit as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Expandable title="Troubleshooting Common Issues" default-open="false">

- **Login fails**: Reset password via the forgot link.
- **Email not verified**: Resend from account settings.
- **Spots not saving**: Ensure no validation errors (e.g., duplicate names).

</Expandable>
