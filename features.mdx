## Overview

Manage your parking operations efficiently with Collaborate To Deliver. You handle parking areas (`Parkbereiche`), track user tasks (`Meine Aufgaben`), monitor vehicles (`Fahrzeuge`), and use visual status indicators to optimize occupancy and maintenance.

<Callout kind="info">
Review these core features and customize them to match your specific parking workflows.
</Callout>

## Managing Parking Areas

Track occupancy levels in dedicated parking zones. Each area shows total spots, current occupancy percentage, and detailed spot views.

| Area     | Location  | Total Spots | Occupancy |
|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Platz 1 | Ingolstadt| 16         | 94%      |
| Platz 2 | München   | 9          | 67%      |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create New Area" icon="plus">
    Click `Neuer Parkbereich` to add a new parking zone. Enter details like location and capacity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Details" icon="eye">
    Select an area to see individual spots numbered 1-16.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update Occupancy" icon="refresh-cw">
    Save changes with `Save All` or cancel with `Cancel All`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning and Tracking Tasks

Keep your team aligned by managing tasks directly in the dashboard.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Assign Task" icon="user-plus">
    Select a team member and assign tasks like inspections or repairs.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Track Progress" icon="activity">
    Monitor `Meine Aufgaben` for open, in-progress, and completed items.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
Prioritize tasks based on occupancy levels to maximize spot availability.
</Callout>

## Monitoring Vehicle Statuses

Use the legend to interpret spot statuses at a glance.

<Expandable title="Status Legend" default-open="true">
  - **Frei**: Available for immediate use
  - **Einsatzbereit**: Ready for deployment
  - **Service fällig**: Service required soon
  - **In Wartung**: Under maintenance
  - **Defekt**: Out of service due to defect
</Expandable>

## Best Practices

- Regularly review occupancy to reassign vehicles from high-occupancy areas.
- Use the `Zurück zur Übersicht` button to navigate between areas quickly.
- Integrate task assignments with status updates for seamless operations.
